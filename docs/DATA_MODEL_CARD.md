# Data and model card

**Purpose.** Research/education and exploratory decision support for overall CAD and vessel-level stenosis. No diagnosis, treatment recommendation, lesion localization, or validated screening decision.

**Data.** Supplied UCI Extension of Z-Alizadeh Sani workbook, DOI 10.24432/C5461K, CC BY 4.0. 303 rows × 59 columns in `Sheet 1 - Table 1`; `Sheet1` is empty and ignored. No missing values or exact duplicate records. 55 eligible input columns; `Exertional CP` is constant. Four feature groups: demographic/history, symptom/examination, ECG, laboratory/echo. Source spelling `Fmale` is preserved, displayed as Female. Numeric categories (including RWMA) are treated as categories, not coordinates. Unit labels follow source conventions; original UCI metadata does not fully document units.

**Labels.** Cath CAD=1 (216), Normal=0 (87); LAD Stenotic=1 (177), Normal=0 (126); LCX positive=119, negative=184; RCA positive=114, negative=189. Source row 94 (one-based) has Cath Normal and LAD Stenotic. The provided labels are preserved independently; none are silently repaired or derived from another target.

**Leakage prevention.** A sklearn `LeakageGuard` runs before the pipeline's transformations during fit and predict. It rejects normalized target names. All four official targets are excluded from every model, including baselines and ablations. The API rejects forbidden, missing and unknown fields. No target labels enter SHAP background or OOD distance. Test fold indices and predictions are persisted for audit.

**Preprocessing.** Inside each fitted partition: numerical median imputation and standard scaling; categorical mode imputation and one-hot encoding with unknown handling. Category vocabularies used to present input controls are a schema inspection artifact, not a fitted global encoder. Class weighting belongs to individual training estimators. No global feature selection, SMOTE or population mixing. XGBoost uses regularization rather than class reweighting. All 55 eligible inputs are retained for schema compatibility.

**Selection and validation.** Fixed seed 2026. Repeated stratified outer 5-fold × 2 repeats; each outer training set runs 3-fold CV over six predeclared candidates: logistic C={0.1,1}; random forest leaf={3,8}, 120 trees; XGBoost depth={2,3}, 110 trees, learning rate .04, lambda=5. Score: inner ROC-AUC - 0.25 × Brier. Pick parameters within each family, then the best family. Outer test labels are unavailable to selection or calibration. All families are also fitted/calibrated within the same training partition for comparison. Final deployed family is selected with the same inner search on all 303 rows, then refitted. Headline evidence evaluates the selection policy, not a single full-cohort fitted model.

**Calibration and thresholds.** Sigmoid predeclared for this small cohort; 3-fold calibration fully inside outer training sets, `ensemble=False`. Raw and isotonic outer-fold comparisons are sensitivity evidence; deployment does not pick the method that happens to perform best on outer test data. Calibrated does not imply perfectly calibrated. 0.50 research classification threshold and color bands <0.30 / 0.30-0.60 / ≥0.60 are display conventions, not clinical categories. Small vessel-model recall at 0.50 is explicitly visible.

**Primary results.** ROC-AUC: CAD .925, LAD .839, LCX .728, RCA .732. Brier: .094, .157, .206, .201 respectively. All accuracy, precision, recall, F1, average precision, confusion matrices, ROC and calibration curves come from repeat-averaged held-out probabilities, one row per patient. Fold mean ± SD is descriptive because folds overlap. 95% patient-stratified bootstrap intervals use 1,000 resamples conditional on fitted folds; they omit retraining/selection uncertainty and do not establish external generalization. See `ml/artifacts/evidence.json` for unrounded results and all OOF values.

**Interpretability.** Approximate permutation SHAP explains the exact calibrated probability. Original categorical variables remain indivisible coalitions. Backgrounds contain only training-partition patients. Local attributions use two permutation cycles and 16 or 20 background patients; base probability plus every contribution reconstructs the output (tested tolerance 1e-6). Global evidence uses six held-out patients × ten outer folds, one cycle each; repeat participation is possible. Mean absolute probability contributions and fold rank Spearman stability are descriptive, not causal or clinical evidence. Correlated inputs can share credit; limited background/permutation sampling produces approximation variance.

**Uncertainty.** Candidate model spread and refitted-model min/max ranges are descriptive. Held-out cases use only the two outer models excluding that row; new profiles use all ten resampled models. These are not calibrated prediction/confidence intervals. OOD is a heuristic standardized mixed-feature nearest-neighbor distance against the source cohort, threshold at 95th percentile of leave-one-out distances, plus per-feature observed range checks. It is not externally validated. Independent targets may disagree and are never forced into a fictitious joint probability.

**Robustness.** Descriptive sex/age subgroup checks, eight largest held-out errors per target, fixed-logistic 5-fold ECG and laboratory/echo ablations. No claims of causal utility, fairness certification or external validation. A deliberately invalid identity predictor shows AUC=1 by construction and is rejected by the guard; it is not trained or deployed.

**Demonstration profiles.** Eight examples are selected by patient characteristics from the source workbook. They are not a representative evaluation sample. Each target's displayed case probability and explanation come from an outer model trained without that row. The range uses both excluding folds. Edits retain the excluded-row model and switch to `held-out sensitivity`, hide original outcomes, and disable exports until prediction runs again. New profiles use full-cohort models. Defaults are cohort medians/modes. BMI is calculated from height and weight; the constant Exertional CP value is fixed. Other fields can be edited.

**Artifacts and security.** Model/dataset hashes, source schema, indices, seed and library versions are recorded. API verifies model SHA-256 before loading trusted local joblib artifacts. Do not load joblib files from untrusted sources. No patient inputs are sent to an external AI service. A bounded in-memory prediction cache holds recent input objects during a local session; no database, request-body logging or storage service. Restart the API to clear that cache. Export/import is an explicit local browser action.

**Limits.** Small single-center referral cohort, high CAD prevalence, no longitudinal outcomes, patient grouping identifiers or independent external cohort. Cannot extrapolate to population screening, another hospital, unmeasured modalities, stenosis severity, exact lesion locations or treatment response. The heart is generic adult male anatomy; no patient-specific imaging is available.

**Final audit clarification.** Repeat-averaged OOF metrics describe an average of two held-out fitted models per patient; they are not an unbiased score for the one full-cohort estimator or the one fold estimator displayed in the workspace. The separate repeat and fold results describe single-fit variability. SHAP additivity validates probability accounting, not attribution accuracy or stability. OOD preprocessing/reference ranges use the whole source cohort; removing the demonstration row from nearest-neighbor lookup does not make OOD a fold-isolated validation result. No OOD accuracy is claimed.

**Prediction identity.** Exported `model_hash` is the SHA-256 of the complete joblib bundle. `model_reference.member` identifies `model` or `folds[index].model` inside it, with training count and SHA-256 of the ordered training-index JSON (compact separators, UTF-8). Source workbook integrity is checked against the registry and evidence before loading models; a changed workbook cannot silently change held-out cases or observed labels.

**Final research operating-point comparison.** One bounded experiment adds ExtraTrees/RBF-SVM to the frozen LCX/RCA nested search, using identical outer splits. Neither clears the predeclared promotion gate; production model hashes and 0.50 headline results remain unchanged. Calibrated inner OOF on outer train selects F1 cutoffs; repeat-averaged probability-minus-cutoff gives one held-out decision per patient. LCX recall/F1 .840/.641, RCA .868/.631; precision .518/.495, with more false positives and lower accuracy. Full-cohort research cutoffs .34/.30 were estimated separately, never substituted into outer evaluation. These are development results on reused splits, not independently confirmed clinical thresholds. See VESSEL_REFINEMENT.md and the complete inner-split/OOF audit.
